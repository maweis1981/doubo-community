# Doubo (都播) — Community & Downloads

Doubo is an IPTV and M3U player for your own playlists. This is the official place to ask questions, share feedback and find public installation packages.

Have a playlist already? [Open the free Web player](https://doubo.tv/en/web/) without an account or installation. Add an M3U/M3U8 channel playlist you are authorised to use, then choose a channel. Stream access and browser compatibility still apply. For native apps, choose your platform under Downloads below.

- **[Questions & FAQ](https://github.com/maweis1981/doubo-community/discussions)** — setup help, playlist questions and maintainer-written answers.
- **[Report a bug or suggest a feature](https://github.com/maweis1981/doubo-community/issues/new/choose)** — include your platform, app version and clear steps.
- **[Official website](https://doubo.tv/)** · **[English guide](https://doubo.tv/en/guide/)** · **[中文指南](https://doubo.tv/guide/)**

## Before posting

Questions belong in **Discussions**. Use **Issues** for a reproducible bug or a concrete feature request. Maintainer-written answers are labelled **Official FAQ**; answers to community questions can be marked as accepted in Q&A.

Everything posted here is public. Keep playlist URLs, provider usernames/passwords, access tokens, payment information and unredacted logs out of posts and screenshots. For a security vulnerability, use [private security reporting](https://github.com/maweis1981/doubo-community/security/advisories/new).

Doubo does not bundle television channels. Use only playlists and content you are authorized to access.
